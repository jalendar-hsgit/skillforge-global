## 📱 Responsive Design

All templates are optimized for:

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Select Template**: Choose from template selector in editor
2. **Fill Data**: Add your work experience, education, skills
3. **Customize**: Change accent color, adjust content
4. **Preview**: Click preview to see full design
5. **Export**: Use "Print/Save as PDF" for high-quality output

## 💡 Design Tips

### Color Psychology:
- **Blue**: Trust, professionalism, stability (Finance, Corporate)
- **Purple/Pink**: Creativity, innovation, modern (Design, Startups)
- **Green**: Growth, balance, health (Healthcare, Sustainability)
- **Orange/Red**: Energy, passion, confidence (Sales, Marketing)

### Template Selection Guide:
- **Entry-Level**: Modern (balanced, professional)
- **Mid-Career**: Elegant Blue (established, credible)
- **Senior/Executive**: Elegant Blue (authoritative, refined)
- **Creative Fields**: Creative (showcase personality, stand out)
- **Tech/Engineering**: Modern (technical, clean, organized)
