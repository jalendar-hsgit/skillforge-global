## 📊 System Overview

### What's Implemented ✅

**Database** (9 Models):
- ✅ Users (with roles: USER, MENTOR, ADMIN, SUPERADMIN)
- ✅ Mentors (1:1 with User for hourly sessions)
- ✅ SellerAccounts (1:1 with User for product sales)
- ✅ DigitalProducts (seller_id FK, status DRAFT/PUBLISHED/SUSPENDED/ARCHIVED)
- ✅ ProductPurchases (tracks buyer, seller, commission split)
- ✅ SellerPayouts (monthly aggregation for wire transfers)
- ✅ MentorSessions (scheduled sessions with pricing)
- ✅ MentorEarnings (payment tracking for sessions)
- ✅ CartItems (temporary shopping cart)

**API Endpoints** (30+):
- ✅ Public: 4 endpoints (list, search, categories, bestsellers)
- ✅ Shopping: 4 endpoints (add cart, view, remove, validate coupon)
- ✅ Checkout: 2 endpoints (create order, confirm payment)
- ✅ Purchases: 2 endpoints (my purchases, verify purchase)
- ✅ Seller: 5 endpoints (create product, edit, list, analytics, payouts)
- ✅ Admin: 8 endpoints (dashboard, products, sellers management)

**Frontend Pages**:
- ✅ /marketplace (product listing with search/filter)
- ✅ /marketplace/cart (shopping cart management)
- ✅ /marketplace/checkout (Stripe payment form)
- ✅ /marketplace/order-confirmation (order details)
- ✅ /admin/marketplace (3-tab dashboard: dashboard, products, sellers)

**Features**:
- ✅ Stripe payment integration (secure card processing)
- ✅ Commission tracking (automatic 30% split for products, 20% for sessions)
- ✅ Admin approval workflow (DRAFT → PUBLISHED)
- ✅ Seller verification (admin must verify before selling)
- ✅ Product suspension (admin can remove products)
- ✅ Email notifications (purchase confirmations)
- ✅ Shopping cart (add/remove items, apply coupons)
- ✅ Analytics dashboard (seller and platform metrics)
- ✅ Payout tracking (monthly earnings, wire status)
- ✅ Duplicate purchase prevention (same user, same product)

### Commission Structure 💰

**Digital Products**: 30% Platform / 70% Seller
- File: `backend/app/api/v1x/marketplace.py` (lines 783, 1792, 1840)
- Why: Marketplace infrastructure, payment processing, content moderation

**Mentor Sessions**: 20% Platform / 80% Mentor
- File: `backend/app/api/v1x/payments.py` (line 135)
- Why: Direct service relationship, lower platform overhead

**Example**:
- Student buys $19.99 product → Platform: $6.00 (30%) + Seller: $14.00 (70%)
- Student pays $75/hour mentor → Platform: $15.00 (20%) + Mentor: $60.00 (80%)
